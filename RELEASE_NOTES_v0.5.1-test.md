## Installing

Copy `dinput8.dll`, `touhou_hfr.dll`, `touhou_hfr.exe` and `touhou_hfr.ini` next to the game's executable. On a Steam copy, press
**Play** in Steam rather than running `touhou_hfr.exe`; `dinput8.dll` installs the patch on
that launch just the same.

New Classic is unchanged: `dxgi.dll`, `touhou_hfr64.dll` and `touhou_hfr.ini` in the `th06nc`
folder, started from Steam. New Classic never had this problem — it loads its patch through a
library the game itself asks for, long after decryption.
